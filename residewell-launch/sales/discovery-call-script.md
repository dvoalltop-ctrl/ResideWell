# Discovery Call Script

**Goal:** 15 minutes. Qualify fit, understand pain, earn permission to send pilot proposal.

**Before the call:**
- [ ] Review property website and CRM notes (5 min)
- [ ] Note 2 specific amenities or observations
- [ ] Have pilot one-pager ready to send immediately after call

---

## Opening (1 min)

> "Hi [First Name], thanks for making time. I'm [Your Name], founder of ResideWell. We work with luxury apartment communities on resident wellness programming and amenity activation — live classes, events, registration, and reporting — so the property team doesn't have to run it.
>
> I've got us down for 15 minutes. Does that still work for you?"

*If they only have 10 minutes: "Perfect — I'll keep it tight."*

---

## Set the Agenda (30 sec)

> "I'd love to learn a bit about how [Property Name] approaches resident experience today, share briefly what we do, and see if an 8-week pilot might make sense. Sound good?"

---

## Discovery Questions (8–10 min)

Ask 5–7 of these. Listen more than you talk.

### Current State
1. "How does your team handle resident events and programming today?"
2. "Who owns that — on-site, regional, or a mix?"
3. "What amenities get the most resident use? Which ones are quieter?"
4. "Do residents ask for fitness classes, yoga, or wellness programming?"

### Pain & Priorities
5. "What's hardest about keeping resident programming consistent?"
6. "Is resident retention or amenity utilization something you're measured on?"
7. "Have you tried fitness classes or wellness events before? How did it go?"
8. "If you could wave a wand, what would resident community look like here?"

### Decision Process
9. "When you bring in a new resident experience initiative, what does approval look like?"
10. "Is there budget for pilot programs, or does everything need to go through corporate?"
11. "Who else would need to be involved in a decision like this?"

### Timing
12. "Is there a season or quarter when programming is especially important?"
13. "If this were a fit, what timeline would you be thinking?"

---

## Bridge to ResideWell (2 min)

> "That's helpful — what you're describing is exactly what we built ResideWell for.
>
> We operate the full programming layer: instructors, scheduling, resident registration, attendance tracking, and monthly reporting. Your team's involvement is light — approve the schedule, share the registration link, and give us space access.
>
> Most properties start with an **8-week pilot** — two classes per week, $4,000 all-in — so you can see attendance and resident response before any annual commitment."

---

## Qualifying Fit

**Green flags (proceed to proposal):**
- Cares about retention, reviews, or amenity utilization
- Has underused spaces (studio, rooftop, lounge)
- Frustrated with inconsistent or staff-heavy events
- Budget authority or clear path to approval
- Timeline within 60 days

**Yellow flags (nurture or educate):**
- Interested but no budget until next fiscal year
- Needs corporate approval — offer materials for internal champion
- Already has part-time programming — position as upgrade/outsourced ops

**Red flags (politely exit):**
- No interest in resident programming at all
- Expects free programming for residents with no budget
- Wants you to pay rent for space
- Property under 100 units or not Class A

---

## Close & Next Step (2 min)

**If qualified:**

> "Based on what you've shared, I think an 8-week pilot could work well — especially using [specific space]. I'll send you a one-page overview and proposal today. Can we schedule 20 minutes next week to walk through it together, or would you prefer to review async and reply with questions?"

**If not ready:**

> "Sounds like timing might be [next quarter / after budget cycle]. I'll send the one-pager so you have it on file, and I'll follow up in [30/60] days. Does that work?"

**Always ask:**

> "Is there anyone else on your team I should include — regional, marketing, or ownership?"
