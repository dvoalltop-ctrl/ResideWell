# Discovery Call Booking

> **Goal:** Every positive reply → **15-minute call on calendar within 48 hours.**

---

## SLAs

| Event              | Deadline                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Prospect replies   | Your response within **4 hours**   |
| They agree to call | Calendar invite within **15 min**  |
| Call completes     | Thank-you + PDF within **2 hours** |
| Qualified lead     | Next step within **24 hours**      |

---

## Before you need this

- [ ] Calendly (free) or Google Calendar appointment slot — 15 min, Tue–Thu 7–8 PM + Sat 9–11 AM
- [ ] Calendar link in a Notes snippet for copy-paste
- [ ] Pilot one-pager PDF ready to attach after the call
- [ ] CRM open — update Status the moment they agree

**Calendar event title:** `ResideWell — [Property Name] — 15 min`  
**Calendar description:** Include property website + CRM notes link.

## Voicemail / Call-Back Booking

If you reach them by phone and they want a call:

> "Great — I'll send you a calendar invite for [day/time] and a quick overview to review beforehand. What's the best email?"

Send invite before you hang up if possible.

---

## No-Show Protocol

1. Wait 5 minutes, text/call once.
2. Email within 1 hour:

**Subject:** Reschedule? — ResideWell / [Property Name]

Hi [First Name],

I had us down for [time] today — no worries if something came up. Happy to reschedule:

[Calendar link] or reply with a better time.

Best,
David Vo

3. CRM: Notes → "No-show [date]", Next Follow-Up → +3 days

---

## After Call Is Booked — Prep (5 min)

- [ ] Re-read property website amenities page
- [ ] Read Outreach Angle + Notes in CRM
- [ ] Prepare 2 discovery questions specific to their property
- [ ] Open `discovery-call-script.md`
- [ ] Pilot PDF ready to attach in follow-up email

**Discovery call is not a pitch deck.** It's 70% listening, 30% pilot overview.
