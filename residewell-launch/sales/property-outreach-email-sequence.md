# Property Outreach Email Sequence

**How to use:** Personalize the `[brackets]` for each property. Send from your ResideWell email. Space emails 3–4 business days apart. Stop if they reply or ask to be removed.

**Subject line options (rotate):**
- Resident experience at [Property Name]
- Quick idea for [Property Name]'s amenities
- 8-week wellness pilot for [Property Name]
- [Property Name] + resident programming

---
