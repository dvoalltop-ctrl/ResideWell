# Phone Call Script

> **Goal:** Reach the property manager → book a **15-minute discovery call**.

**When:** After Email 2 with no reply, or when you have a name but no email.  
**Best times:** Tue–Thu, 10–11 AM or 2–3 PM.

---

## Call flow

| Step       | Time   | What to say                  |
| ---------- | ------ | ---------------------------- |
| 1. Opening | 10 sec | Ask for PM by name           |
| 2. Reason  | 20 sec | ResideWell + [Property Name] |
| 3. Qualify | 60 sec | 2–3 questions                |
| 4. Close   | 15 sec | Ask for 15-min call          |

## Opening Line

> "Hi, this is [Your Name] with ResideWell. Is [First Name] available?"

**If front desk answers:**

> "Hi, I'm [Your Name] with ResideWell — we partner with luxury apartment communities on resident wellness programming. I'm trying to reach [Community Manager Name / the community manager]. Is [he/she/they] available, or is there a better time to reach them?"

---

## Reason for Calling (when connected)

> "Hi [First Name], I'm [Your Name], founder of ResideWell. I'll be brief — I know you're busy.
>
> We help Class A apartment communities activate amenity spaces with live wellness classes and resident events — registration, instructors, reporting — without adding work to your team.
>
> I was researching properties in [Submarket] and [Property Name] came up. I sent a quick email [earlier this week / last week] but wanted to reach out directly in case it got buried."

---

## Qualifying Questions (pick 2–3)

1. "How does your team handle resident events and programming today?"
2. "Is amenity utilization or resident retention something you're focused on this year?"
3. "Do you have spaces — a studio, rooftop, or lounge — that could work for wellness classes?"
4. "Have you ever run fitness or yoga classes for residents?"

---

## How to Ask for the Property Manager

**If front desk won't transfer:**

> "No problem — could you tell me the community manager's name so I can send a note to the right person?"

**If they say "email leasing@...":**

> "Thank you — is there a direct email for the community manager, or should I address it to the leasing team?"

**If manager is unavailable:**

> "When is usually the best time to catch [First Name] — mornings or afternoons?"

---

## How to Ask for a 15-Minute Call

> "I don't want to take more of your time on the phone. Would it make sense to schedule a quick 15-minute call this week or next? I can send a one-page overview beforehand so you know exactly what we do.
>
> I have [Tuesday at 10] or [Thursday at 2] open — either work for you?"

**If they're interested but busy:**

> "Totally understand. Can I send you a one-pager and follow up by email? What's the best address?"

**If they say send info:**

> "Absolutely — I'll send it today. Would it be okay if I follow up in a few days to see if you have questions?"

---

## Voicemail Script (under 30 seconds)

> "Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] with ResideWell. We help luxury apartment communities run resident wellness programming — live classes, events, and reporting — without adding work to the property team.
>
> I was reaching out about [Property Name] and sent a quick email as well. My number is [phone]. I'd love 15 minutes to see if an 8-week pilot might be a fit. Thanks, [First Name] — talk soon."

_Speak slowly. Repeat your name and number once._

---

## Objection Quick Responses (on the phone)

| They say               | You say                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "We're not interested" | "Totally fair — can I ask what you use for resident programming today, just so I don't reach out again unnecessarily?"                          |
| "Send me information"  | "Will do — I'll email a one-pager today. What's the best email? And is it okay if I follow up in a few days?"                                   |
| "We already do events" | "That's great — a lot of our partners did too. We usually complement that by handling the wellness layer consistently. Worth a 15-minute look?" |
| "No budget"            | "We start with a small 8-week pilot at $4,000 — designed to prove ROI before any annual spend. Would that be worth a conversation?"             |

_Full objection handling: see `objection-handling.md`_
