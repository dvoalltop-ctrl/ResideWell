# Founder Weekly Schedule (Full-Time Job + ResideWell)

Realistic schedule for building ResideWell while employed.full-time. **~10–12 hours/week** focused on revenue activity, not branding.

**Principles:**
- Mornings = outreach and research (before work brain gets hijacked)
- Evenings = calls and follow-ups (Tue–Thu only)
- Weekends = batch research and one deep work block
- **No infinite website tweaking.** Ship, sell, iterate.

---

## Weekly Template

### Monday
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:00–6:45 AM | Research 3 properties → add to CRM, score, find contacts |
| 6:45–7:00 AM | Send 3 personalized outreach emails |
| Evening | Off — recovery day |

**Weekly total Monday:** ~1 hr

---

### Wednesday

### Sunday
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 7:00–7:30 PM | Plan next week: pick 15 outreach targets, 5 calls, 1 proposal goal |
| 7:30–8:00 PM | Light CRM cleanup — Next Follow-Up dates, stale leads |

**Weekly total Sunday:** ~1 hr

---

## Total: ~10–12 hours/week

---

## Priority Stack (when time is tight)

1. **Discovery calls** — never cancel for research
2. **Proposal follow-ups** — revenue is here
3. **New outreach to High-priority CRM leads**
4. **Phone calls to non-responders**
5. **Property research** — feed the pipeline
6. **Instructor recruiting** — needed before pilot, not before first discovery call
7. **Website/branding** — only after first proposal sent

---

## What NOT to Do This Week

- [ ] Rebuild the website for the third time
- [ ] Design a logo for 4 hours
- [ ] Build a custom CRM app
- [ ] Research 100 properties with zero outreach
- [ ] Recruit 50 instructors before you have a signed pilot

---

## First Pilot Countdown Checklist

When you have a verbal yes:

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Week -2 | Sign agreement, collect deposit, confirm schedule |
| Week -2 | Assign instructors, property walkthrough |
| Week -1 | Registration link live, property promotes |
| Week 0 | Kickoff call, first class |
| Week 4 | Mid-pilot check-in |
| Week 8 | Final report, annual conversion conversation |

---

## Energy Management

- Protect sleep — early mornings only work if you're in bed by 10 PM.
- One evening call block max per night.
- Saturday deep work is optional if you're burned out — shift to Sunday planning only.
- First paid pilot > perfect process.
