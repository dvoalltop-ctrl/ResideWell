## CRM Rules (Week 1)

1. **No outreach without `Contact Verified = Yes`** (email verified or LinkedIn conversation started).
2. **Every email needs a `Personalization Hook`** — one specific sentence about their property.
3. **Use 6 statuses only** — see `crm/crm-field-definitions.md`.
4. **Sort by Quick Score** — contact 4–5 scores first.
5. **Next Follow-Up Date** on every row you've touched — no exceptions.

## When Someone Replies

| They say | You do | File |
|----------|--------|------|
| "Tell me more" | Send 3-bullet reply + 2 call times | `discovery-call-booking.md` |
| "Send info" | Send one-pager PDF + propose call times | `discovery-call-booking.md` |

| "Call me [day]" | Calendar invite immediately | `discovery-call-booking.md` |
| No reply after Email 1 (4 days) | Email 2 | `property-outreach-email-sequence.md` |
| No reply after Email 2 (4 days) | Phone call | `phone-call-script.md` |
