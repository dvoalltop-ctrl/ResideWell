# Monthly Client Report Template

> **Who owns this?** Founder.
> **When does it happen?** Within 5 business days of month end (and at pilot weeks 4 and 8).
> **What tools/files are needed?** `class-attendance-tracker.csv`, resident feedback responses, this template, `../ai-workflows/monthly-reporting-prompts.md` to draft faster.
> **What does "done" mean?** One-page PDF delivered to the property contact, forward-ready for their regional or ownership.
> **What can go wrong?** Missing attendance rows, overselling weak numbers, report arriving late and losing renewal momentum.
> **What is the backup plan?** Attendance is submitted within 2 hours of every class (instructor standard), so the data always exists. If numbers are soft, report honestly with an adjustment plan — credibility wins the annual.
