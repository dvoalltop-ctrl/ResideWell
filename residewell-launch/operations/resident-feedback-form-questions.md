# Resident Feedback Form Questions

Use after week 4 and week 8 of the pilot. Google Forms works well. Keep it under 2 minutes to complete.

**Form title:** [Property Name] Wellness Class Feedback  
**Form description:** We'd love your feedback on resident wellness classes at [Property Name]. This takes under 2 minutes and helps us improve programming.

---

## Questions

1. **Which class did you attend?** _(multiple choice)_
   - Yoga
   - Pilates
   - Strength / HIIT
   - Mobility / Stretch
   - Other

2. **Which instructor led the class?** _(dropdown — list instructor first names)_

3. **Overall, how would you rate this class?** _(scale 1–5 stars)_

4. **How likely are you to attend another class at [Property Name]?** _(scale 1–5)_
   - 1 = Not likely
   - 5 = Very likely

5. **What did you enjoy most?** _(long answer, optional)_

6. **What would you change or improve?** _(long answer, optional)_

7. **What other class formats would you like to see?** _(checkboxes)_
   - Yoga (gentle / flow)
   - Pilates
   - Barre
   - Strength / HIIT
   - Stretch / mobility
   - Meditation / breathwork
   - Outdoor / rooftop classes
   - Social wellness events (sound bath, workshop)
   - Other: ___

8. **What days and times work best for you?** _(checkboxes)_
   - Weekday morning
   - Weekday evening
   - Saturday morning
   - Sunday morning

9. **How did you hear about this class?** _(multiple choice)_
   - Email from property
   - Resident portal
   - Lobby flyer
   - Social media
   - Friend / neighbor
   - Other

10. **Any additional comments for the property or ResideWell team?** _(long answer, optional)_

---

## Closing Message

> Thank you for your feedback. Your input helps [Property Name] and ResideWell bring you programming you'll actually use. See you at the next class.

---

## How to Use Feedback

| When                 | Action                                                      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Week 4               | Send form — adjust week 5–8 class mix if clear pattern      |
| Week 8               | Send form — include quotes in final report                  |
| 4+ star comments     | Ask permission to use in marketing (with property approval) |
| Recurring complaints | Address with instructor or schedule change immediately      |

---
