# ResideWell Launch Sprint — This Week, Day by Day

> **One goal:** Book discovery calls with luxury apartment property managers → close the first **$4,000 paid 8-week pilot**.

**The offer (memorize it):** 8-week paid pilot · 1 live class per week · launch marketing · registration QR code · attendance tracking · feedback survey · final engagement report · annual recommendation · **$4,000** (~$2 per unit per week at a 250-unit community).

---

## The week at a glance

| Day             | Focus                  | Output                                                |
| --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Day 1 (Mon)** | Setup + first outreach | CRM live, 3 properties researched, 3 emails sent      |
| **Day 2 (Tue)** | Outreach + LinkedIn    | 3 emails, 3 connections, replies handled              |
| **Day 3 (Wed)** | Outreach + follow-ups  | 3 emails, Email 2s out, 2 LinkedIn messages           |

**Weekly targets:** 15 emails · 10 LinkedIn touches · 5 calls · **2 discovery calls booked** · 1 proposal sent.

## Day 1 (Monday) — Setup + first sends (~2 hr)

1. **CRM (20 min):** `crm/week-1-crm-setup.md` → copy `crm/target-properties-week1.csv` into Google Sheets as `ResideWell Pipeline`. Add Status dropdown + 3 filter views.
2. **Research 3 properties (45 min):** `crm/property-research-scorecard.md` — public data only, 15 fields, Fit Score 1–5, mark unknowns "Unknown."
3. **Verify contacts (20 min):** LinkedIn for your top Fit-5 properties. No verified contact = no email.
4. **Send 3 Email 1s (15 min):** `sales/property-outreach-email-sequence.md` — paste each row's Outreach Angle.
5. **Export the one-pager (15 min):** `proposal/8-week-pilot-one-pager.md` → Google Docs → replace brackets → save as `ResideWell-8-Week-Pilot.pdf`.
6. **Website once (10 min):** `website/index.html` — replace `[Your Name]`, phone, email. Then close it for the week.

## Day 2 (Tuesday) — Outreach + LinkedIn (~1 hr + evening slot)

Run `sales/daily-outreach-workflow.md`:

- Answer replies first (4-hour SLA) → `sales/discovery-call-booking.md`
- 3 Email 1s · research or verify 2–3 properties
- LinkedIn: 3 connection requests (`sales/linkedin-outreach-sequence.md`)
- 7:00–7:30 PM: discovery call slot (hold it free)

## Day 3 (Wednesday) — Outreach + follow-ups (~1 hr + evening slot)

- Replies → follow-ups due → 3 Email 1s
- Email 2 to Monday non-responders (4-day gap)
- LinkedIn: 2 follow-up messages
- Evening: discovery call or proposal follow-up

## Day 4 (Thursday) — Outreach + phone (~1.25 hr)

- Replies → follow-ups → 3 Email 1s
- **2 phone calls** to Fit-5 non-responders (`sales/phone-call-script.md`)
- **If 10+ emails sent:** instructor recruiting opens — `instructors/instructor-recruiting-workflow.md` (form live, 5 messages max)
- Evening: discovery call slot

## Day 5 (Friday) — Close the week (~1 hr)

- 3 Email 1s → 15 for the week
- Email 2 to Tuesday non-responders
- CRM audit: every active row has Status + Next Follow-Up Date
- Fill `operations/weekly-execution-dashboard.md` — grade the week on **discovery calls booked**

## Day 6 (Saturday) — Optional (90 min max)

Pick one: batch research 5 properties · discovery call prep · proposal customization. **Never:** website redesign, logo, new tools.

## Day 7 (Sunday) — Plan (30 min)

- Pick 15 targets for next week (Fit Score 5s and 4s, Contact Verified = Yes)
- Confirm calendar slots for discovery calls
- Read `operations/week-1-playbook.md` if you drifted

## Guardrails

- **Do not** build apps, databases, or automations — the CSV + Sheets is the system.
- **Do not** invent contacts or unit counts — "Unknown" until verified.
- **Do not** recruit 20 instructors — 5 messages after 10 property emails.
- **Do not** discuss annual pricing before a pilot exists.
- **Do** send 3 emails every weekday. Volume + follow-up wins.

**Friday scoreboard: discovery calls booked. Everything else is supporting activity.**
