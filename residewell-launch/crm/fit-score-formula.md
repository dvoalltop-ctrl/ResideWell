# Fit Score Formula (1–5)

> One number that answers: **how hard should I chase this property?** Enter it in the `Fit Score` column. Score during research (see `property-research-scorecard.md`).

---

## The rubric

| Score | Definition                                                                                     | Action                                   |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **5** | Luxury, 180+ units, strong amenities, professional management, likely resident engagement need | Verify contact today → Email 1 this week |
| **4** | Strong fit but missing **one** key signal                                                      | Email 1 after all 5s are contacted       |
| **3** | Possible fit — needs more research                                                             | Park; revisit when pipeline thins        |
| **2** | Weak fit                                                                                       | Skip unless portfolio angle              |
| **1** | Do not prioritize                                                                              | Remove or archive                        |

---

## The five signals

Count how many of these are clearly true (public data only):

| #   | Signal                      | Clearly true when…                                                                         |
| --- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | **Luxury positioning**      | Class A branding, premium rents, upscale photography                                       |
| 2   | **Scale**                   | 180+ units (150–179 = half credit)                                                         |
| 3   | **Strong amenities**        | Fitness center **plus** at least one activation space (studio, rooftop, courtyard, lounge) |
| 4   | **Professional management** | Named third-party or institutional manager                                                 |
| 5   | **Engagement need**         | Underused spaces, sparse events calendar, or reviews asking for community                  |

**Scoring:**

- All 5 signals → **Fit Score 5**
- 4 signals → **Fit Score 4**
- 3 signals → **Fit Score 3**
- 2 signals → **Fit Score 2**
- 0–1 signals → **Fit Score 1**

---

## Worked example

> 240-unit Class A lease-up in Alpharetta, Greystar-managed, rooftop + fitness center, Instagram shows two events in six months.

| Signal                  | Verdict           |
| ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| Luxury positioning      | ✔                 |
| Scale (180+)            | ✔                 |
| Strong amenities        | ✔                 |
| Professional management | ✔                 |
| Engagement need         | ✔ (sparse events) |

**Fit Score: 5 → High priority, contact this week.**

---

## Rules

- Score with public evidence, not hope. Unknown signal = not true.
- Unit count unverified? Mark `[VERIFY]` in the CSV and use your best public source for scoring.
- Priority Level maps directly: 5 → High, 4 → Medium, 3 → Low, 1–2 → skip.
- Re-score if new information arrives (e.g., a discovery call reveals budget or a portfolio).
