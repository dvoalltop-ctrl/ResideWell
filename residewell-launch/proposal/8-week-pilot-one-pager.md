Luxury apartment communities invest heavily in amenities — fitness centers, studios, rooftops, lounges — but most see declining utilization after move-in season. Resident programming is inconsistent, staff bandwidth is limited, and ownership lacks clear data on whether amenities drive retention and satisfaction.

Your team shouldn't have to become event planners, instructor coordinators, and marketers on top of everything else.

## The Solution

This is not a gym membership. It is structured resident experience programming designed to activate amenities and strengthen community.

## Success Metrics

We measure pilot success on:

1. **Weekly attendance** — target: 8–15 residents per class (varies by property size)
2. **Registration rate** — % of residents who sign up at least once
3. **Repeat attendance** — residents who return for 2+ classes
4. **Property team satisfaction** — minimal lift, clear communication
5. **Qualitative feedback** — resident comments and review themes

You'll receive a written report at week 4 and week 8 with these metrics and a recommendation for next steps.
