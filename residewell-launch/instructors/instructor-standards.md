# Instructor Standards

Every ResideWell instructor represents the property and our brand. These standards are non-negotiable for pilot and annual programs.

---

## Punctuality

- Arrive **minimum 20 minutes before** class start (30 minutes preferred for first class at a new property).
- Complete setup before residents arrive — music, mats, equipment, signage if applicable.
- If running late, notify ResideWell immediately — never the property manager first.
- Repeated lateness (2+ instances) = removal from roster.

---

## Professionalism

- Dress: clean, fitted athletic wear appropriate for a luxury residential environment. No offensive graphics.
- Language: welcoming, inclusive, no profanity.
- Boundaries: friendly with residents, not overly personal. No soliciting private clients on-site without ResideWell approval.
- No phone use during class except for music/timer.
- Leave space clean — equal to or better than you found it.

---

## Music

- Use resident-appropriate playlists — upbeat but not aggressive. Match format (yoga ≠ HIIT energy).
- Volume appropriate for residential setting — confirm with property if outdoor/rooftop.
- Bring own speaker unless property provides one (confirm in advance).
- No explicit lyrics.

---

## Resident Interaction

- Welcome every resident by name when possible (check registration list).
- Offer modifications without making anyone feel singled out.
- Encourage without pressuring — residents are here to enjoy, not perform.
- Handle complaints or injuries calmly — notify ResideWell same day.
- Do not discuss property management issues, rent, or leasing with residents.

---

## Safety

- Begin with brief safety note and invitation to modify.
- Ask about injuries privately if someone is new — don't call out in front of group.
- No hands-on adjustments without consent.
- Know emergency exits and AED location (confirm at property walkthrough).
- Incident protocol: resident injury → ensure safety → notify property contact + ResideWell immediately → document in writing within 24 hours.

---

## Class Setup & Breakdown

**Before class:**
- [ ] Space cleared and safe
- [ ] Mats/equipment laid out or accessible
- [ ] Music tested
- [ ] Registration checked — welcome walk-ins only if policy allows
- [ ] Sign-in sheet or digital check-in ready

**After class:**
- [ ] Equipment returned and stored
- [ ] Space wiped down if needed
- [ ] Trash removed
- [ ] Lights/locks per property instructions
- [ ] Attendance submitted to ResideWell within 2 hours

---

## Communication

- Primary contact: ResideWell operations — not the property manager for schedule changes.
- Respond to ResideWell messages within **4 hours** on business days.
- Sub requests: minimum **48 hours notice** unless emergency. ResideWell approves subs.
- Weekly schedule confirmation required by **Friday noon** for following week.

---

## Red Flags (immediate removal)

1. No-show without notice
2. Teaching under influence of alcohol or drugs
3. Harassment or inappropriate contact with residents
4. Ignoring safety incident
5. Soliciting residents for private business repeatedly
6. Sharing property access codes or resident information
7. Repeated complaints from property or residents

---

## Payment

- Instructors invoice ResideWell biweekly or per agreed terms.
- Payment contingent on: attendance submitted, standards met, no unresolved incidents.
- Rate agreed in writing before first class — no mid-pilot renegotiation without ResideWell approval.

---

## Instructor Agreement (summary for onboarding)

By teaching for ResideWell, instructors agree to:
- Maintain current certification and liability insurance
- Follow all standards in this document
- Represent ResideWell and the property professionally
- Provide 48-hour notice for schedule conflicts
- Allow ResideWell to obtain feedback from properties and residents

*Full contractor agreement to be signed before first paid class.*
