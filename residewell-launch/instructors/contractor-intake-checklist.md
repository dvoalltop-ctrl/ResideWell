# Contractor Intake Checklist

> **Who owns this?** Founder.
> **When does it happen?** After an instructor passes screening and before their first paid class.
> **What tools/files are needed?** This checklist, `instructor-standards.md`, contractor agreement, W-9 form, insurance certificate.
> **What does "done" mean?** Every box below checked, documents filed, and the instructor confirmed in writing for their full 8-week schedule.
> **What can go wrong?** Missing insurance on class day, rate disputes mid-pilot, no-shows with no sub, tax paperwork missing at year end.
> **What is the backup plan?** No instructor teaches without insurance on file — swap in the bench instructor. Rate and schedule live in the signed agreement, not memory.

## 1. Documents (before first class — no exceptions)

- [ ] Signed independent contractor agreement (scope, rate, 8-week term, termination)
- [ ] W-9 collected and filed
- [ ] Certificate of liability insurance received — verify dates cover the full pilot
- [ ] Certification copy on file (RYT-200, NASM, Pilates cert, etc.)
- [ ] Rate confirmed in writing: **$______ per class** ($65–$85 standard range)
- [ ] Payment method + invoicing cadence agreed (biweekly recommended)

## 2. Standards & expectations

- [ ] Instructor has read `instructor-standards.md` and confirmed by reply
- [ ] Punctuality: arrive 20–30 min early — acknowledged
- [ ] Music conduct: resident-appropriate, no explicit lyrics — acknowledged
- [ ] Safety: modifications offered, incident protocol understood — acknowledged
- [ ] No soliciting residents for private business — acknowledged
- [ ] Communication: 4-hour response window, ResideWell first (never the property) — acknowledged

## 3. Backup coverage

- [ ] Named backup/sub identified: _______________
- [ ] 48-hour minimum sub notice agreed (emergencies excepted)
- [ ] Backup has been screened or is on the ResideWell bench

## 4. Property logistics

- [ ] Space guide sent (photos, parking, entry, setup, breakdown)
- [ ] Building access method confirmed and tested
- [ ] Emergency contacts exchanged (property + ResideWell)
- [ ] First-class date confirmed in writing: _______________
- [ ] Full 8-week schedule confirmed in writing

## 5. Day-one readiness

- [ ] Attendance submission method explained (within 2 hours of class)
- [ ] Photo/marketing consent noted (yes / with notice / no)
- [ ] Emergency contact for instructor on file
