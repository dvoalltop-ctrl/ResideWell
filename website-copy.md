## Problem

**Section label:** The Challenge

**Headline:** Beautiful amenities do not create engagement on their own.

**Body:** Luxury properties invest heavily in fitness centers, rooftops, lounges, pools, and courtyards. But onsite teams are already stretched, resident events can be inconsistent, and amenity spaces often go underused.

**Problem cards:**

1. **Underused amenity spaces** — Premium spaces sit quiet between move-in season and the occasional resident event.
2. **Inconsistent resident programming** — Wellness initiatives start strong, then fade when staff bandwidth shifts.
3. **Too much work for onsite teams** — Property teams are not hired to plan classes, recruit instructors, and track attendance.
4. **Limited reporting on engagement** — Ownership asks for resident experience data — and teams lack clear numbers to share.
5. **Residents want community, not just access** — Luxury renters expect connection and programming, not only a key fob to the gym.
