# ResideWell Sales Copy

When writing ResideWell sales copy:

- Write to property managers, regional managers, and multifamily operators.
- Lead with resident engagement, amenity activation, operational ease, and retention.
- Do not lead with "fitness classes."
- Use simple, professional language.
- Keep emails concise.
- Make the next step low-friction: 15-minute call, quick walkthrough, or 8-week pilot.
- Avoid hype, buzzwords, and overpromising.
- Use per-unit pricing when helpful.
- Mention that ResideWell handles programming, instructors, resident marketing, registration, attendance tracking, and reporting.

## Preferred phrases

- Amenity activation
- Resident engagement
- Turnkey wellness programming
- Low-lift for your onsite team
- Underused amenity spaces
- Resident experience
- Renewal support
- Class A communities
- 8-week paid pilot

## Avoid

- Gym business
- Workout vendor
- Fitness side hustle
- Cheap classes
- Bootcamp-only positioning
